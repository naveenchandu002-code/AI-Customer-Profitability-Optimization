# Final Resume Project

## Customer Profitability & Decision Optimization Platform

- Built an end-to-end customer decisioning platform for **10,127 credit-card customers**, combining **Python, SQL, XGBoost, SHAP, and Power BI** to translate customer behavior into targeted retention and growth decisions.

- Developed an **out-of-fold XGBoost churn model** and customer-value framework, then integrated prediction with intervention economics; identified **229 customers with positive modeled retention economics**, avoiding a churn-only targeting strategy.

- Built a **budget-constrained optimization engine** that selected the highest-value retention targets; under a **$10,000 modeled budget**, selected **100 customers** and generated **$5,387 modeled incremental value (53.87% modeled ROI)**, outperforming random targeting across tested budget scenarios.

### Interview Note

Financial outcomes are **modeled scenario estimates** because the public dataset does not contain actual bank revenue, servicing costs, campaign-response outcomes, or retention-offer costs.
